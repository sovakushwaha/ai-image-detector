Important scientific limitation
-------------------------------
Perceptual hashing can identify some visually similar images but cannot guarantee detection of all source-content overlap.
It may miss:
- substantial crops
- large transformations
- semantically related recreations
- heavily altered images
Therefore this is an additional leakage check, not proof that the dataset contains no leakage.
