SCIENTIFIC PURPOSE
------------------
Train: learn model parameters.
Validation: model/feature/hyperparameter/threshold development. Do not use test sets for these decisions.
Known test: evaluate held-out images from familiar generators (ADM, BigGAN, GLIDE, SD15).
Unseen test: evaluate cross-generator generalisation using generators never exposed during model development (Midjourney, VQDM, Wukong).

SOURCE LIMITATION
-----------------
Source-level independence cannot be guaranteed from official metadata.
Tiny-GenImage does not provide true source IDs.
Filename-derived ImageNet-like tokens are not used as official source IDs.
SHA-256 duplicate screening found no exact duplicates.
pHash screening found no confirmed near-duplicate copies.
These checks reduce leakage risk but do not prove complete source independence.

RAW vs controlled_v1
--------------------
split_assignments_v1.csv is keyed by image_id (and original_filename / raw_path).
The same assignments can be applied to RAW and controlled_v1. Those two experiments must differ by preprocessing only, not by sample identity.
